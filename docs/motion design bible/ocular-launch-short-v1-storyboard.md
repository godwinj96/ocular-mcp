## Narrative in one line

An AI finds a button by _seeing_ it — then we learn it was blind all along, watch perception ignite, watch it act with vision, and pull back to see how much world there is to see.

## Loop posture

Because `loop: true`, the composition opens _and_ closes on the Conviction frame (§II.11 rule 8: field, subject, and motion parity). The story therefore reads as a **departure and return**: we begin in lucidity, fall into blindness, and climb back. A viewer who arrives mid-cycle sees the same arc; a viewer who watches twice experiences the ending as the beginning of a re-explanation, which is exactly the posture rule 9 describes. The Conviction frame is also the poster frame (§II.11 rule 11), so the pre-playback still is already the loop's resting state.

## Beat-by-Beat Storyboard

### B0 — REST (0.0–0.8 s) · `L3-lucid` · loop anchor

**Stage.** The Conviction frame, held: Ocular White field (`n-900`), Ink wordmark centered, absolutely still. Motion spend 0.0.

**Why it exists.** This is the loop's fixed point — identical to frame 599 (§II.11 rule 8), and the poster frame. It is also the composition's thesis stated _before_ it is argued: we begin at lucidity so the fall into blindness has somewhere to fall from.

**Boundary out — Lucidity descent.** The single sanctioned pre-Discovery awareness regression (§II.8 rule 2 permits `L0↔L1` oscillation pre-Discovery; this is the loop-anchor exception introduced by DDR-021's field-parity requirement): the field travels `L3 → L1` over `t-scenic` with `e-shift` as the wordmark exits by conjugate Emergence. Light drains and the world we will spend the video re-earning goes dim.

---

### B1 — HOOK (0.8–3.6 s) · `L1-aware`

**Stage.** The Persistent Field settles at `L1`: Ocular Ink environment, soft directional key, 3:1–5:1 contrast. Center column: an Idealized UI webpage (checkout page — neutral grays from the Ink↔White axis), already present. No entrance; we arrive mid-function (Inversion Rule — the Product Atom is working before the viewer finishes arriving).

**Action.** A gaze ring — a thin circular focus indicator, the agent's attention made visible — glides along a gently curved path to the page's "Place order" button (`t-deliberate`, `e-observe`; this is the Observer's Attention Cycle in miniature: anticipate, attend, confirm). On arrival: `t-micro` press state. The button was _found by looking_, not by code.

**Type.** Claim enters via the sanctioned per-word Accelerating Stagger (the composition's single Recognition reveal): "AI" · "can" · "write," · "code," · "reason." — intervals 8→7→6→5→4 frames (`t-swift` → `t-micro`). Dwell: `t-deliberate` + 5×80 ms ≈ 44 frames of stillness.

**Budget.** Gaze glide = primary (0.7). Claim stagger begins only in the glide's deceleration phase. Ambient: faint field drift (0.1).

---

### B2 — BLIND (3.6–7.6 s) · `L1 → L0-blind`

**Boundary in (Field Handoff, 32 f overlap).** Claim exits (`e-exit`, `t-swift`); as it leaves, the light drains.

**Stage.** The same page — but awareness regresses to `L0` (permitted pre-Discovery): 15–25% luminance, ≤2:1 contrast, and critically, **flat** (Depth Law: flatness is the pre-perception state). The rendered page de-renders: its layout collapses into a monochrome DOM tree — raw element tags, text nodes, a wireframe of angle brackets. This is what the agent actually "sees."

**Action.** Minimal motion (tension is stillness + dimness, not chaos): a plain cursor traverses the DOM text, hesitates at a `<div>`, backtracks. One subordinate motion (0.3). The hesitation _is_ the story.

**Type.** "But it browses blind." — Emergence, `t-base`. Long dwell. This is the emotional floor of the composition.

---

### B3 — SIGHT (7.6–11.9 s) · **the Discovery event** · `L0 → L2`

**Boundary in = Lucidity Step.** The Discovery event: the single `t-monumental` (128 f / 4.27 s) illumination rise, `e-shift`, beginning one Anticipation Lead (6 f) _before_ B2's claim has fully exited — light arrives as if it knew.

**Action.** As luminance climbs `L0 → L2`: the flat DOM gains volume — layers separate into depth (comprehension is volumetric), the wireframe resolves _through defocus into focus_ (Resolution reveal) as the actual rendered page, now lit, dimensional, and sharp. The gaze ring reappears at the exact spot the cursor had hesitated — and settles on the button instantly. What was a search is now a glance.

**Type.** "Ocular gives AI vision." — Emergence, `t-deliberate`, entering only in the illumination rise's deceleration phase. This claim receives the longest dwell of any in the composition.

**Budget.** The Discovery event is the primary (0.7); the Resolution of the page rides inside it as its object. Nothing else moves. This is the composition's single largest event and must feel like it.

---

### B4 — ACTION (11.9–15.8 s) · `L2-perceiving` · Decrescendo begins

**Boundary in (Field Handoff).** Claim exits; the seen page persists.

**Action.** Three gaze-actions, strictly sequential (never parallel), each shorter-_held_ than powerful — and per the Decrescendo Principle, each followed by a **longer** hold than the last:

1. Gaze ring finds a price field → reads it (highlight pulse-free; a focus settle). Hold 12 f.
2. Gaze ring finds an empty form field → text fills (`t-swift`). Hold 18 f.
3. Gaze ring finds the confirm button → click → success state (Emergence, `t-micro`). Hold 26 f.

Each action is one primary; each begins in the previous action's deceleration phase (Motion Budget rule 3).

**Type.** "It sees. It acts." — Emergence, `t-base`, present across all three actions (one Claim per Beat).

---

### B5 — WORLD (15.8–20.0 s) · `L2 → L3-lucid` · Conviction · loop close

**Boundary in (Field Handoff into camera move).** The claim exits; the Observer Camera performs the composition's only large move: a dolly-out (`t-scenic`, `e-observe`) — dolly-out signifies comprehension of scale — revealing the checkout page as one of a receding field of pages, tabs, and interfaces: the entire web, seeable.

**The final step.** As the dolly settles, the `L2 → L3` step completes the Polarity arc: the field crosses from Ink toward White. The page-field gently exits (`e-exit`) as luminance crests — the world dissolves into lucidity rather than darkness.

**Conviction frame (534–600, 2.2 s).** Ocular wordmark — Poppins Bold, Logomark O with the 40° Catchlight — in **Ocular Ink, centered on Ocular White**, entering by the **light-pole Sigil Reveal** (§I.6 rule 7, per DDR-018): the mark sits at ≤ 8% contrast as the field crests toward white, the Catchlight resolves from defocus, and the mark darkens into full legibility while the world brightens. Then absolute stillness. Total motion spend ≤ 0.1.

The last thing the viewer experiences is the language's thesis — stillness, brightness, certainty — and, because the loop wraps here, it is also the first thing. Frame 599 must match frame 0 exactly: same wordmark position, scale, and focus; same `n-900` field; zero motion in flight.

---

## Decrescendo Compliance (III.14 rule 9)

Integrated per-Beat motion spend: B3 (maximum — the Discovery event) → B4 (three staggered primaries, each briefer) → B5 (one camera move → stillness). Monotonically non-increasing post-Discovery ✓. Holds: 12 f → 18 f → 26 f → 66 f terminal ✓ monotonically non-decreasing.

## Gap Log (discovered by this derivation → all closed in v0.5.0)

1. **Sigil Reveal polarity gap.** I.6 rule 7 defines the Sigil Reveal against an `L0` field (white-variant logo, near-invisible ring). The Conviction frame is `L3` (Ink-on-White), so the canonical logo entrance as specified cannot occur at the canonical logo moment. **RESOLVED — DDR-018:** the Sigil Reveal is now contrast-relative with dark-pole and light-pole instances; B5 uses the light-pole instance as specified.
2. **Gaze ring is unregistered.** The agent's visible attention indicator is used here as the Product Atom's protagonist but is not a canonical entity. It is arguably Ocular's most important recurring visual — the product's perception made visible. **RESOLVED — DDR-019:** registered in Brand Constants §I.6 rules 10–14, with Logomark-derived geometry, Attention-Cycle behavior, and the L0-absence rule that makes B2's ringless cursor canonical rather than improvised.
3. **Success-state grammar.** **RESOLVED — DDR-020:** §I.6 rule 15 — state changes use luminance step, focus resolution, and the Gaze Ring's confirm settle only. B4's success state is now a luminance step plus confirm settle.

## Prohibited patterns (explicit, for implementers)

This composition must contain none of the following. Each is canon-prohibited and each appeared in the prior website video, which this replaces:

- Spring, bounce, elastic, or press-release easing on any element, especially the logo (§I.2 Law 7, §II.10 rule 4)
- RGB-split, glitch, datamosh, or scanline effects to signify the blind state (§IV.15 anti-patterns — blindness is absence, not malfunction)
- Particle bursts, sparkles, or glow flashes on success/connection (§I.6 rule 15, §IV.15 anti-patterns)
- Grain, dot-grid, vignette, or any field texture (§IV.15 rule 3)
- Wordmark letters translating, orbiting, or assembling; the Logomark rotating or pulsing (§I.6 rule 8, §II.12 rule 6)
- Accent colour used for hierarchy or state (§I.6 rule 3, §IV.15 rule 2)
- More than one Gaze Ring active at once (§I.6 rule 14); any Gaze Ring at `L0` (§I.6 rule 13)
